# FinGuard-GRC — Known Limitations & Future Research Roadmap

> While `FinGuard-GRC v1.0` establishes a reproducible end-to-end baseline for financial IT governance analytics, enterprise deployment in regulated Hong Kong institutions introduces deeper statistical, graph-structural, and regulatory challenges. This document explicitly states the current system's methodological boundaries alongside six concrete research and engineering directions for subsequent iterations.

---

## Part 1: Known Limitations & Methodological Threats to Validity

| Dimension | Current Limitation in v1.0 | Practical Impact / Threat to Validity | Addressed By |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Synthetic Telemetry Generalization** | The 3,600-event dataset is generated by a controlled stochastic simulator with deterministic seed (`42`). | Real enterprise SIEM/IAM exports (Okta, CyberArk, SWIFT) exhibit missing values, schema drift, and multi-system clock skew. | Direction 1, Direction 3 |
| **2. Compact Regulatory Corpus Scope** | The offline knowledge base models `18` representative clauses across HKMA TM-G-1 / C-RAF, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and NIST AI RMF 1.0. | Full regulatory circulars span hundreds of pages with nested legal cross-references and synonym variation beyond lexical/TF-IDF matching. | Direction 4 |
| **3. Uncalibrated Tree Margins & Seasonal Shift** | `HistGradientBoosting` operating threshold (`0.33`) is F2-tuned on Days 54–66 without post-hoc Isotonic/Platt probability calibration or drift monitoring. | Quarter-end regulatory reporting or annual audit cycles can shift feature distributions, inflating false-positive rates if thresholds remain static. | Direction 1 |
| **4. Single-Event Tabular Boundary** | Features evaluate each access event independently (`row`-level). | Cannot detect **multi-hop transitive toxic entitlements** (e.g., *User → Role A → Group B → Service Account → SWIFT*) across identity graphs. | Direction 2 |
| **5. Heuristic Remediation ROI Valuation** | The What-If ROI Simulator applies a constant expected-loss assumption (`HK$18,500` per remediated violation). | Different controls (e.g., *JIT PAM Checkout* vs. *90-Day Recertification*) yield heterogeneous causal risk reduction across departments. | Direction 5 |

## Part 2: Six Future Research & Optimization Directions

### 1. Temporal Concept Drift Detection & Probability Calibration *(Addresses Limitations #1 & #3)*
- **Motivation**: Financial IT access patterns exhibit strong seasonality (quarter-end regulatory reporting, annual external audits, bonus/payroll cycles).
- **Planned Approach**:
  - Track **Population Stability Index (PSI)** and **Kolmogorov–Smirnov (KS)** drift statistics across top risk drivers (`records_exported_log`, `sod_rule_score`, `peer_group_volume_zscore`).
  - Apply **Isotonic Regression / Platt Scaling** on the validation split (`Days 54–66`) so `ml_probability` reflects calibrated empirical risk probabilities rather than raw tree margin scores.

### 2. Graph Neural Network (GNN) Modeling for Multi-Hop Toxic Entitlements *(Addresses Limitation #4)*
- **Motivation**: Tabular feature engineering captures pairwise Separation of Duties (`sod_rule_score`) and department mismatches, but misses transitive privilege paths across roles and service accounts.
- **Planned Approach**:
  - Model employees, roles, groups, and enterprise systems as a heterogeneous **Identity & Entitlement Bipartite Graph**.
  - Train a **GraphSAGE / Relational GCN** link-anomaly detector to surface multi-hop privilege escalation paths and benchmark PR-AUC against tabular `HistGradientBoosting`.

### 3. Active Learning & Closed-Loop Retraining from Human Audit Reviews *(Addresses Limitation #1)*
- **Motivation**: FinGuard-GRC already logs immutable auditor dispositions (`Confirmed Violation`, `False Positive`, `Escalated to CISO`) in SQLite `audit_reviews`. However, naive retraining on only reviewed alerts introduces **selection bias** because low-scoring false negatives are rarely reviewed.
- **Planned Approach**:
  - Implement **uncertainty sampling** (routing borderline `0.40–0.60` probability events) combined with **random low-score audit strata** (e.g., 2% random sample of `Low` tier events) to construct an unbiased feedback dataset for quarterly model retraining.

### 4. Dense Semantic Retrieval & Cross-Encoder Reranking for Full Regulatory PDFs *(Addresses Limitation #2)*
- **Motivation**: The current offline hybrid **BM25 + TF-IDF** retriever achieves `100% Recall@3` (`0.9514 MRR`) on 24 paraphrased queries over 18 concise clauses, but scaling to full HKMA SPM and SFC GenAI PDFs requires semantic understanding across long legal paragraphs.
- **Planned Approach**:
  - Index chunked regulatory PDFs with local sentence-transformer dense embeddings + **Cross-Encoder reranking**, and introduce automated **citation faithfulness & groundedness checks** before presenting remediation steps to auditors.

### 5. Causal Uplift Modeling for IT Governance Remediation ROI *(Addresses Limitation #5)*
- **Motivation**: IT leadership needs to know *which* control intervention yields the highest marginal risk reduction per engineering hour rather than relying on a uniform expected-loss constant.
- **Planned Approach**:
  - Apply **Uplift Modeling / Causal Forests (EconML / DoWhy)** on historical remediation interventions to estimate the Conditional Average Treatment Effect (CATE) of each control type per department.

### 6. Privacy-Preserving Cross-Subsidiary Audit Analytics (HK PDPO)
- **Motivation**: Multi-subsidiary financial groups operating across Hong Kong and Greater Bay Area jurisdictions face strict **Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (PDPO)** and cross-border data residency constraints that prohibit centralizing raw employee/KYC logs.
- **Planned Approach**:
  - Explore **Federated Learning** (aggregating gradient-boosted or neural risk weights across subsidiaries without centralizing raw PII) paired with **Differential Privacy** guarantees on shared governance benchmarks.
